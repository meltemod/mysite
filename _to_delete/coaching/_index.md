I am [Meltem Odabaş](/), a social scientist with over a decade of experience in academic research, applied policy work, and data-driven initiatives. My background spans university settings, nonprofit organizations, and research institutions, where I have led projects on public opinion, evaluation, global health and social policy. This dual perspective, combining rigorous academic training with real-world application, positions me to support researchers navigating the transition from academia to applied careers.

## About This Mentorship

Transitioning from social science MA/PhD programs or early academic roles to applied research positions can be challenging. Many researchers excel in theoretical and methodological work but face gaps in practical guidance for alt-ac paths, such as policy analysis, nonprofit leadership, or data science roles in research organizations. This mentorship provides structured support to gain strategic clarity, build professional networks, and develop the skills needed for successful transitions.

## Who This Is For

This mentorship is designed for:

- MA/PhD students in social sciences
- Postdocs and early-career researchers
- Individuals exploring or pursuing alternative academic (alt-ac) careers

You may be asking yourself:

- How do I translate my research skills to applied roles?
- What does a career outside academia look like for me?
- How can I build a professional network in policy or nonprofits?
- What skills do I need to develop for leadership in applied settings?
- How do I negotiate job offers and long-term growth in these fields?

## What We Work On

Our sessions focus on practical strategies for career transitions:

- Clarifying career direction and identifying applied opportunities
- Transitioning from academic to applied research environments
- Positioning social science skills for policy, nonprofit, or data roles
- Developing strategic thinking beyond research execution
- Building job search strategies and negotiation skills
- Planning long-term professional trajectories in alt-ac paths

## My Approach

This is strategic mentorship, not therapy or generic advice. Drawing from systems thinking and real-world experience across academia and applied research, sessions are structured, reflective, and action-oriented.

My approach is grounded in understanding your goals, constraints, and decision context, while maintaining a focus on forward-moving discussions that support clarity, direction, and intentional next steps.
