This study adapts the Adding It Up methodology to generate subnational estimates of sexual and reproductive health (SRH) needs, costs, and impacts across four Nigerian states. By localizing the evidence, the analysis supports state-level planning and investment decisions for contraception, maternal and newborn care, abortion services, and STI treatment.

I contributed to the data analysis and production of this study as part of the research team at the Guttmacher Institute.
