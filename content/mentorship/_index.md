Transitioning beyond academia is not just a job search problem. It is a positioning problem.

Many social scientists have strong analytical and methodological skills but lack clarity on how to translate them into applied roles. This mentorship focuses on building that clarity, repositioning your experience, and helping you move into research roles across policy, nonprofit, and data-driven organizations.

I am [Meltem Odabaş](/), a quantitative social scientist and research strategist with over a decade of experience across academia, public-interest research, and applied data science. I have worked at organizations such as the Guttmacher Institute and Pew Research Center, leading research that informs policy and funding decisions.

I mentor social scientists navigating the transition from academia to applied research careers.

## Who This Is For

This mentorship is designed for:

- MA/PhD students in social sciences
- Postdocs and early-career researchers
- Social scientists exploring or preparing for alternative academic (alt-ac) careers

You may be asking yourself:

- How do I translate my research skills to applied roles?
- What direction should I be moving toward? 
- What does a career outside academia look like for me?
- How do I position myself for leadership and long-term growth?

## What We Work On

Our sessions focus on practical strategies for career transitions:

- Clarifying career direction and identifying realistic opportunities
- Repositioning academic experience for applied research roles
- Building a strong narrative for your profile, CV/resume, and applications
- Positioning social science skills for policy, nonprofit, or data roles
- Developing strategic thinking beyond technical execution
- Structuring your job search and decision-making process
- Preparing for interviews, offers, and negotiation

By the end of this process, you will have:

- A clear and grounded career direction
- A strong and coherent professional narrative
- A concrete strategy for your next steps

## My Approach

This is strategic mentorship, not therapy or generic advice.

Our work is structured, reflective, and focused on action. We begin by clarifying your goals, opportunities, and constraints, and move toward concrete decisions and steps you can take.

The goal is to help you navigate this transition with clarity, confidence, and direction.
