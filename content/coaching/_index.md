# Coaching for Early Research Professionals

Building a career in research can feel intellectually exciting — and professionally uncertain. Many early-career researchers are highly skilled technically but lack structured support in navigating direction, positioning, and professional growth.

You may be asking:

- What direction am I actually moving toward?  
- How do I grow beyond technical execution?  
- How do I position myself for leadership?  
- Where does my work fit long-term?  

I work with undergraduate and graduate students, postdocs, and early-career research professionals who want to build rigorous work and intentional careers — in academia, nonprofits, policy institutions, or applied research settings.

## What we work on

Our conversations focus on clarity, strategy, and professional growth. Topics often include:

- Clarifying long-term career direction
- Transitioning between academia and applied research
- Developing strategic thinking beyond technical execution
- Communicating effectively with stakeholders and leadership
- Clarifying professional identity and leadership voice
- Negotiating roles, expectations, and compensation
- Building sustainable research workflows

## My Approach

With over a decade of experience leading research initiatives and building analytical systems, I bring both technical depth and strategic perspective to our sessions.

Coaching is structured, reflective, and forward-moving. It is not therapy and not generic career advice. It is a focused space to think clearly, make decisions intentionally, and move with confidence.

I am currently completing Level 1 ICF-accredited coach training and working toward Associate Certified Coach (ACC) credentialing.
